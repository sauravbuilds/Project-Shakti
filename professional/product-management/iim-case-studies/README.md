# IIM Case Studies

**Path:** `professional/product-management/iim-case-studies/`

Reports on official IIM case studies. Not a summary of the case, a record of what I actually concluded from it.

## Format

- **Case** → what the case was about, briefly
- **My take** → the decision I would have made and why
- **What I'm carrying forward** → what changes in how I'd approach a similar real situation
