## Intellectual

### MFT Exam

- Cleared the MFT exam.
- The exam was harder than I expected.
- I had prepared around 600 curated questions using AI.
- In hindsight, I think my previous preparation method would have worked better:
  - Take the syllabus topic by topic.
  - Go through each topic using AI.
  
- I also learned through lectures, but some of them had become outdated because SBI regularly introduces new regulations.
- I had read the newer regulations, which helped me solve some questions based on the updated rules.
- However, the exam also had many questions that required deeper understanding of the syllabus.

### Exam Experience

- I expected to clear the exam, but I was not 100% confident going in.
- Roughly:
  - ~20% of the questions were answered largely through intuition because I had never properly covered that part of the syllabus.
  - ~20% were from topics I was familiar with and had practiced before, but I was not clear about the specific concept being tested.
  - The remaining questions were from areas I had prepared more directly.
- Overall, I was skeptical about whether I would clear it because of the difficulty.
- I cleared it.

### Learnings

- Question banks alone are not enough for this type of exam.
- For future exams, I should prioritize complete syllabus coverage and conceptual understanding before practicing large numbers of questions.
- I need to learn more about mutual funds in depth.
