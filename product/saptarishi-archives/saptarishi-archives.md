# Saptarishi Archives

This is the context for Saptarishi Archives, the current product under the Product pillar. Use this whenever discussing, building, or writing about this product.

*A research-driven digital archive — built to be the most comprehensive evidence-based record of India's civilizational heritage ever assembled, covering every domain, every era, every contribution — documented with verified sources, academic references, and zero speculation.*

## The Oldest Story Never Told

India is one of the last continuously living civilizations on earth. The decimal system. The zero. Sushruta's surgical techniques, documented two millennia before Europe caught up. Pāṇini's grammar, a framework so precise that modern linguists still cannot fully replicate it.

These did not emerge from nowhere. They came from this land. What the world calls "progress" has Indian fingerprints all over it, and almost none of the credit.

## It Is Still Happening

Right now, multinationals are walking into global markets with Indian craftsmanship repackaged as their own innovation. Kolhapuri chappals, a GI-tagged sandal with centuries of lineage, are sold as a "new silhouette." Jhumkas, documented in temple sculpture and Mughal manuscripts, appear in luxury retail as a fresh design trend, with their origins unmentioned.

This is not inspiration. It is the quiet, profitable erasure of origin, done in plain sight, with full legal cover, because the original inventors were never loud enough to stop it.

## Our Standard

No claim without documented proof. Every investigation is backed by at minimum one peer-reviewed or archival source before publishing.

## Source Types We Accept

1. **Academic** — Peer-reviewed journals, university press publications, doctoral theses.
2. **Archival** — Archaeological surveys, ASI records, government GI registrations.
3. **Primary** — Historical manuscripts, temple iconography, museum collections with provenance.
4. **Legal** — Patent filings, court records, GI tag databases, IP tribunal rulings.
