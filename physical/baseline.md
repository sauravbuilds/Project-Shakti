# Training Philosophy

Although my training history has been inconsistent, I have always tried to approach progressive overload conservatively.

I prioritize **correct form and controlled progression. I do ego lift sometimes to test my strength, but never with weights I cannot control.**

For example:

* For squats, I would increase weight only after completing **3 sets × 10 reps** comfortably at the current weight.
* For barbell movements, once the weight became significantly heavier, I would move toward **8-rep sets** rather than sacrificing form.
* Whenever returning after a break, I generally restarted around **70-75% of my previous working strength**.

Because of this approach and my previous training exposure, I have retained a reasonable amount of strength despite long breaks.
