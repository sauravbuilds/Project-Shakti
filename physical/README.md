## Tracking System

> *"Organized by month. Each file is named after the date it covers."*

Every day will have a separate entry documenting the physical state and activity of that day.

### Daily Entry Format

* **Workout** - exercises performed, sets, reps, and relevant strength numbers
* **Macros & Micros** - food consumed and nutritional breakdown
* **Mental Notes** - energy, mood, recovery, and anything else worth noting

The goal is not to collect unnecessary data. It is to build a simple, consistent record that makes physical progress easy to track over time.
