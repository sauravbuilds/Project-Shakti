**I built a company and then killed it, on purpose.**

At Valuebound, I owned the 0 to 1 build of Problock.ai. Talked to 22 medical reps and 4 pharma leaders myself.

**I started a company on my own.**

saptarishiarchives.com
 
It's a website where I'm archiving everything about India. Not just contributions to the world, but the cultural and heritage side too.
 
This one goes back a while for me.
 
In school, our textbooks barely mentioned any Indian scientist or thinker who contributed anything to the world. So for a long time I genuinely believed India hadn't contributed much.
 
Later I found out that wasn't true. A lot of it just never made it into what we were taught.
 
I've been thinking about this, on and off, since 9th grade. First I wanted to make a YouTube channel about it. Now I think a website is the better way to actually do it properly, with real sources, not just videos.
 
What actually got me to start building it in 2026 is seeing Indian culture get appropriated in real time. Yoga, various cultural and heritage practices, taken and repackaged without ever naming India or giving credit.
