## The Five Pillars

---

### 01. Physical

**Path:** `physical/2026/`

Organized by year → month → date. Each file is named after the date it covers.

Each daily entry logs:
- **Workout** → exercises done that day
- **Macros & Micros** → what I ate and the nutritional breakdown
- **Mental notes** → how I felt, energy levels, anything worth noting

Simple. Consistent. Compounding.

More years will be added as they come.

---

### 02. Intellectual

**Path:** `intellectual/`

Every book, podcast, article, and meaningful conversation documented. Nothing is counted unless it is finished and reviewed.

Each entry follows a fixed format:
- **Summary** → what it is about in my own words
- **Key Takeaways** → what I am actually taking away and carrying forward

**What lives here:**

- `books/` → literary & genre fiction, non-fiction / science, business & economics, philosophy & psychology, biography & history
- `podcasts/` → flat, occasional audiobooks live here too
- `articles/` → anything worth documenting from the web
- `observations/` → what I learned by meeting someone in person, or by paying attention

This is not a reading list. It is a record of what I have actually read.

---

### 03. Professional

**Path:** `professional/`

This is career leverage, everything that makes me materially better at the work I actually do and ship for a living. Two equal legs: **AI Engineering** and **Product Management**. Neither is subordinate to the other. The bet is that a PM with real depth in AI, able to build actual agentic products and not just prompt one, gets exponential leverage most PMs and most engineers can't touch alone.

**`ai-engineering/`**: a structured, block-by-block roadmap to being able to build and ship agentic AI systems on my own. Scoped deliberately: enough software engineering to build and deploy real products end-to-end, not a software-engineering degree. Every block builds on the previous one. Every block ends with a hands-on build.

| Block | Focus |
|---|---|
| Block 01 | Python + Pydantic |
| Block 02 | Prompt Engineering + RAG + GenAI |
| Block 03 | Agents + LangGraph + MCP |
| Block 04-05 | Shipping: FastAPI, Docker, Databases, Cloud (minimum viable deploy, not SRE) |
| Block 06 | Testing + Observability + Evals + 3 Products |

Also lives here: `ai-engineering/automation/`, automating real workflows, primarily with Claude. It's an application of AI engineering, not a separate domain.

NOTE: If you're following this, don't copy the notes. Write your own, paste them into any LLM, and ask it to write notes in a similar style. It will be more useful for you.

**`product-management/`**: the craft I already practice daily as a PM in fintech, tracked with the same rigor as everything else in this repo.

- `mfd-exam/` → prep and progress toward the exam
- `frameworks/` → PM frameworks studied and actually applied
- `decisions/` → real calls made at work: what was decided, why, and what happened (anonymized where needed)

Same rule as everywhere else in this repo: **actions don't count, progress does.** A finished course means nothing on its own — what matters is the before/after. Could bench 70kg, now 72kg. Knew Python basics, now shipped something with it. Every entry here follows that same shape: baseline → what changed → evidence of progress.

---

### 04. Product

**Path:** `product/saptarishi-archives/`

No code lives here. This pillar is a progress log. What was worked on, what decisions were made, what features were added, what changed and why.

**Current Product:** [Saptarishi Archives](https://www.saptarishiarchives.com)

A research-driven digital platform investigating cultural appropriation and documenting the authentic historical origins of Indian civilizational contributions. Evidence-first. Proof before publish. In favour of the original inventors. Truth, not narrative.

More products will be added to this pillar as they are built.

---

### 05. The Report

---

>> *"A moving man surely meets his luck, the road favours the traveller."*

---

**Path:** `the-report/2026/`

The layer that sits on top of the other four pillars. Raw logs mean nothing until they are turned into a decision. This pillar exists so that happens on a fixed cadence instead of never.

- **Monthly Report** *(mandatory)* → one file per month, every month, even if a pillar had zero activity that month. A blank month is logged, not skipped.
- **Weekly Report** *(when possible)* → a shorter checkpoint, every Sunday, to catch drift before a month has already passed.

---

## How This Repository Gets Updated

- Every book, podcast, or article finished → entry added to `intellectual/`
- Every day trained → log pushed to `physical/`
- Every topic, chapter, or technology completed → pushed to `professional/ai-engineering/`
- Every PM concept studied, framework applied, or real decision made → pushed to `professional/product-management/`
- Every meaningful product milestone → documented in `product/`
- Every month (mandatory) and every week (when possible) → reported in `the-report/`

One entry at a time, when it is earned.
